Rescued from bonded labour, but still waiting years for help? The Supreme Court has now set clear deadlines ⛓️⚖️

A worker and others were rescued from a brick kiln in Shahjahanpur, UP, in February 2019 and sent home to Bihar. 42 of them got release certificates, but only 25 received the immediate aid of Rs 20,000 each. The petitioner got his money nearly three years later, and 17 workers were still waiting.

The Court said freedom does not end with the rescue. The State's duty under Articles 21 and 23 continues until the worker can live with dignity. Complaints should normally be checked within 24 hours, the release certificate issued within 24 hours of deciding the person was bonded, and immediate financial help paid within 7 days of the certificate. A rehabilitation plan must start within 90 days of rescue. Help cannot be held back just because the police case is still pending (if the scheme conditions are met), and a dispute between two States over who pays cannot delay it.

Every district must have a grievance officer to settle complaints, normally within 30 days. UP and Bihar must verify the 17 pending cases within 1 month and pay within 6 months at the latest. The Centre must also decide within 3 months on raising the aid amount.

Case: Surendra Manjhi & Anr. v. Union of India & Ors., W.P. (C) No. 511 of 2022, 2026 INSC 1185, 2026 LiveLaw (SC) 1121 (2 Nov 2026)
Bench: Justice Vikram Nath and Justice Aparesh Kumar Singh
