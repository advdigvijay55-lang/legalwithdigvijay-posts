Waiting endlessly at a government office in Delhi? A new law now puts a price on delay ⏱️⚖️

The Delhi government has notified the Delhi (Right of Citizens to Time-Bound and Easy Delivery of Services) Act, 2026, which replaces the 2011 law. It gives citizens a statutory right to get covered government services within a fixed time. The government will notify which services are covered, the maximum number of days for each, and the officer responsible. It applies to Delhi government departments, local bodies and notified institutions.

You can apply online and track your application with a unique number. If it is rejected, the reason must be given in writing. An officer who delays without justification can be fined Rs 250 per day, up to Rs 5,000, and wrongful rejection can attract a fine of Rs 250 to Rs 5,000. The officer must be heard before any fine. Keep in mind: the fine goes to the government treasury, not to the applicant.

If the deadline is missed, you don't need to file a fresh appeal. Your application is automatically treated as an appeal to the Citizen Grievance Redressal Authority, which normally decides within 30 days. Unresolved matters go to the Delhi Right to Service Commission. Watch for the official list of covered services and timelines.

Law: Delhi (Right of Citizens to Time-Bound and Easy Delivery of Services) Act, 2026 (notification announced 9 Nov 2026)
