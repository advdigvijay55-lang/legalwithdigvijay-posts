Drug user = victim, sirf criminal nahi? ⚖️

Supreme Court ne Telangana ki ek college student ke khilaf NDPS case quash kar diya. Aarop tha ki usne college ke paas ganje ka packet liya — lekin uske paas se kuch baramad hi nahi hua, aur case sirf co-accused ke bayan aur confession par tika tha.

Court ne kaha: nasha karne wale log drug chain ke aakhri sire par hote hain. Unhe jail mein daalne se woh drug trade mein aur gehre ja sakte hain. Asli focus supply chain todne par hona chahiye.

📌 Yaad rakhein: police ke saamne kiya gaya confession court mein saboot nahi hota (Section 23 BSA). Aur NDPS mein consumption aaj bhi apradh hai — yeh faisla nasha legal nahi karta.

Case: Sanjana Bhaskaran v. State of Telangana, 2026 LiveLaw (SC) 1044
Bench: Justice J.B. Pardiwala & Justice K. Vinod Chandran
