UPI par ab charge lagega? 🤔 Sach yeh hai 👇

15 October 2026 se NPCI ka naya MDR framework lagu ho raha hai. Rs 2,000 se upar ke kuch merchant (P2M) UPI payments par 0.4% fee lagegi — maximum Rs 300. Railways, telecom, insurance, fuel jaisi categories mein flat Rs 5.

Lekin yeh fee merchant bharega, aap nahi. Merchant ise customer par daal nahi sakta. Dost/family ko paisa bhejna (P2P) har amount par free hai, aur UPI apps koi platform fee nahi le sakti.

📌 Kanoon kya kehta hai: Finance Ministry ne 14 Sept 2026 ki notification S.O. 5067(E) se Rs 2,000 tak ke UPI payment aur RuPay debit card payment par bank/system provider ke har direct ya indirect charge par rok laga di hai — Payment and Settlement Systems Act, 2007 ke Section 10A ke tahat.

Source: NPCI FAQ (15 Sept 2026) · MoF notification S.O. 5067(E) (14 Sept 2026)
