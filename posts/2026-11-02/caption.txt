Planning to burst crackers this Diwali in Delhi-NCR? Read the Supreme Court's rules first 🪔⚖️

On 2 November 2026, the Supreme Court allowed only certified green crackers in Delhi-NCR, and only on 7 and 8 November, from 6 AM to 7 AM and from 8 PM to 10 PM. The full ban returns on 9 November. The court made clear this is a temporary relaxation, not the end of the ban.

Only CSIR-NEERI approved green crackers in boxes with a QR code are allowed. Ladis (crackers joined in a series), garlands, barium crackers and online sales are not allowed. No crackers within 300 metres of hospitals, nursing homes, health centres, old-age homes and notified silence zones, and educational institutions are also to be marked as no-cracker zones.

Police must check sale points, verify QR codes, seize illegal crackers and prosecute offending makers and traders. The court warned officials of contempt action if its order is not enforced, and said religious freedom is subject to public health and the right to clean air under Article 21.

Case: Arjun Gopal & Ors. v. Union of India & Ors., 2026 LiveLaw (SC) 1115 (2 Nov 2026)
Bench: Justice MM Sundresh and Justice PB Varale
