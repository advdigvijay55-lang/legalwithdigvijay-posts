Bought or sold a second-hand vehicle? Read this before an accident happens 🚜⚖️

A tractor-trailer carrying sand overturned and its driver died. The tractor had already been sold, but the insurance policy was still in the old owner's name. When the family claimed compensation, the insurance company said it had no customer and no cover.

The Andhra Pradesh High Court disagreed. Under Section 157(1) of the Motor Vehicles Act, when a vehicle changes hands, its insurance policy is treated as transferred to the new owner. The policy was valid on the day of the accident, and a name not updated is only a technical objection that cannot defeat the family's claim. The award of Rs 2,91,684 was upheld.

Tip: the law still asks the buyer to apply to the insurer within 14 days of the transfer to update the name. Do it, so there is no dispute later.

Case: Royal Sundaram Alliance Insurance Co. Ltd. v. Purama Raghavamma & Ors., C.M.A. No. 486 of 2010
Bench: Justice Balaji Medamalli, Andhra Pradesh High Court
