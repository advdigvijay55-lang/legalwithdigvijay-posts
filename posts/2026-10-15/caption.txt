Is a birth or death in your family still not registered? Read this 📜⚖️

From 1 October 2026, the Registration of Births and Deaths (Amendment) Act, 2026 has made late registration stricter. If the event is reported more than one year but within two years late, you need an order from the DM, SDM or an Executive Magistrate authorised by the DM.

If it is more than two years late, only a Judicial Magistrate First Class of the area where the birth or death took place can order the registration. In both cases the facts are verified and a prescribed fee is charged. The government says the aim is to encourage timely reporting.

Why it matters: a birth or death certificate is accepted as evidence of that birth or death, and it gives a person legal identity. If you already have a certificate, you do not need a new one. If a record in your family is still missing, do not wait.

Law: Registration of Births and Deaths (Amendment) Act, 2026 (Act 12 of 2026), amending Section 13(3) of the 1969 Act. In force from 1 Oct 2026.
