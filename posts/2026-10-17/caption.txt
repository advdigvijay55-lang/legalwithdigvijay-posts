Got a maintenance order but the money still isn't coming? You are not alone, and the law has answers 💰⚖️

Myth 1: "The order is enough." No. If payments stop, you must file an execution application to enforce it. The Allahabad High Court has now told every trial court in Uttar Pradesh to decide such applications within six months, following the Supreme Court's 2025 ruling in Periyammal. Ignoring this may amount to contempt.

Myth 2: "I need a new case every time he misses a month." No. A maintenance order is a continuing duty. The High Court has held that a wife does not have to file a fresh execution plea for each default, and the case cannot be closed just because one month's dues were paid.

Myth 3: "If he refuses, nothing can be done." No. Courts can send the money straight to the claimant's bank account, ask an employer to deduct it from salary, attach property and, as a last step, order simple imprisonment of up to one month for each month's default.

Cases: Data Ram v. State of U.P., 2026 LiveLaw (AB) 851 | Mala Kumari v. State of U.P., 2026:AHC:152860
Bench: Justice Praveen Kumar Giri, Allahabad High Court
