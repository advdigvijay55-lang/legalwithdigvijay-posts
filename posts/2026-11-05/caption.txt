Wages late or work denied under the rural jobs scheme? There is now a 15-day deadline to resolve your complaint 🧑‍🌾⚖️

On 2 November 2026, the Ministry of Rural Development notified the VB-G RAM G Grievance Redressal Rules, 2026, under the VB-G RAM G Act, 2025 — the new rural employment law that replaced MGNREGA. Any person can complain about any part of how the scheme is run.

You can complain in person to the grievance officer, by speed post, on the online portal or through a helpline. Paper complaints must be put online the same day or by the next working day, and you get an acknowledgement with a registration number. A complaint cannot be refused because of its format, language or the way it was sent. The enquiry must be finished within 7 working days and the complaint resolved within 15 days. If it is not, it moves up automatically to the next level, and you can track it online.

If you are not happy with the decision, you can appeal to higher officers under Schedule I of the Act (45 days to file, 30 days to decide). Some safeguards in the May 2026 draft, like penalties for delay, were left out of the final rules, but duties already in the Act still apply.

Law: VB-G RAM G Grievance Redressal Rules, 2026, Ministry of Rural Development, notified 2 Nov 2026
