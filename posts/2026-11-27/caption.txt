Can a death from work pressure be treated like a workplace accident? The Madras High Court says yes 🏭⚖️

A 51-year-old helper loading cotton at a textile mill in Theni collapsed on 16 November 2016 after stress and high blood pressure from heavy work. She was declared brought dead. The Commissioner awarded her family Rs 5,32,800 with 12% yearly interest: Rs 3 lakh from the insurer under the mill's group accident policy, the rest from the mill.

The insurer appealed. It said there was no external injury and she already had BP, so this was a normal death, not an accident. Justice P. Murugan disagreed. She died during her employment because of work pressure and heavy work, which is covered by Section 3(1) of the Employees' Compensation Act, 1923. The lack of a visible injury alone cannot defeat the claim.

The Court upheld the award and the 12% interest under Section 4A(3), and dismissed the appeal. Remember, each case depends on its facts, and the link between the work and the death must be shown.

Case: The Branch Manager v. N. Pitchaimani & Anr., CMA (MD) No. 619 of 2023, 2026 LiveLaw (Mad) 562
Court: Madras High Court, Madurai Bench, Justice P. Murugan
