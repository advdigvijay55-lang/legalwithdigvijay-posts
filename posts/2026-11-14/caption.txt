Married, earning or living separately? You may still claim road accident compensation for a family member 🚗⚖️

Myth: "A married daughter, an earning spouse or a sibling who lives apart cannot claim anything." The Delhi High Court has said this is wrong. Such a family member cannot be excluded just because they are married, employed or live separately. Every legal representative of the deceased can file a claim under Section 166 of the Motor Vehicles Act without first proving financial dependency.

The case: a woman lost her father and sister in a 2015 road accident. She had married years earlier and lived with her own family. The tribunal treated her as a non-dependent and gave her only a reduced amount, and the insurer argued she was not dependent at all. The High Court said dependency cannot be put into fixed boxes and must be judged on the facts. Indian families often share a common family income, and a member living elsewhere may still draw support from it.

The court held she was partly dependent and raised her compensation by over Rs 12 lakh, with 9% interest. Remember, dependency still affects the share: a fully dependent member should get more than a partly dependent one, and the tribunal decides the amount in each case.

Case: United India Insurance Co. Ltd. v. Savita & Anr., 2026:DHC:9920
Bench: Justice Anish Dayal, Delhi High Court (reported 12 Nov 2026)
