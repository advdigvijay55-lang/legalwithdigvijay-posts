Buying gold? Check for the hallmark first 💍🔍

The Centre has extended compulsory hallmarking of gold jewellery to 398 districts across 26 States and Union Territories, up from 392. The six districts newly added are West Champaran (Bihar), South West Delhi, Panchmahal (Gujarat), Bulandshahr and Sant Kabir Nagar (Uttar Pradesh), and Rudraprayag (Uttarakhand). Uttar Pradesh now has 41 districts on the list. The change applied from its publication in the Gazette on 3 November 2026.

In listed districts, registered jewellers must sell gold jewellery and artefacts only with a BIS hallmark. A hallmark has three parts: the BIS logo, the purity grade (for example, 22K916 means 22 carat) and a six-character HUID code unique to that piece. You can enter the HUID in the free BIS CARE app to check the purity, the jeweller and the testing centre. If a hallmarked piece tests below its stamped purity, the buyer is entitled to twice the value of the shortfall plus the testing cost.

Some items are exempt even in listed districts, such as pieces under 2 grams, gold bullion, articles meant for export, and jewellers with a yearly turnover of up to Rs 40 lakh. Always ask for a bill showing the net gold weight, purity and hallmarking charge.

Law: Hallmarking of Gold Jewellery and Gold Artefacts (Fourth Amendment) Order, 2026, S.O. 5997(E) dated 2 November 2026, issued under the BIS Act, 2016
Issued by: Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food and Public Distribution, with BIS
