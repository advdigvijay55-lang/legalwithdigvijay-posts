Your car's pollution certificate may now last up to 3 years 🚗🌿

The Ministry of Road Transport and Highways has notified the Central Motor Vehicles (Sixteenth Amendment) Rules, 2026, which change Rule 115(7) of the Central Motor Vehicles Rules, 1989. The rules came into force on publication in the Official Gazette. How long a PUC (Pollution Under Control) certificate stays valid now depends on three things: the vehicle's emission norm, its age from the date of first registration, and whether it is a private or transport vehicle.

For BS-VI vehicles up to 6 years old, the PUC is valid for 3 years if the vehicle is private and 2 years if it is a transport vehicle. From over 6 up to 10 years, it is valid for 1 year, and after 10 years, only 6 months. BS-IV vehicles get 6 months, and BS-I, BS-II and BS-III vehicles get just 3 months.

If you renew within the 10 days before your certificate expires, the new one starts from the old expiry date. If you renew late, the new validity counts from the renewal date. Your existing certificate stays valid until it expires; the new periods apply from your first renewal after the change.

Source: Central Motor Vehicles (Sixteenth Amendment) Rules, 2026, amending Rule 115(7), Central Motor Vehicles Rules, 1989, under the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988 (reported 19 November 2026)
