Soon, your milk packet will carry a new logo 🥛✅

The Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI) has notified the Food Safety and Standards (Labelling and Display) Second Amendment Regulations, 2026. Under this change, all milk and milk products, including composite milk products, must carry one standard logo: a white milk drop inside a blue square, printed on the main front panel of the pack.

FSSAI says the aim is to make it simpler for consumers to readily identify products in this category. The logo has a minimum size based on the front panel area, from 6 mm on the smallest packs to 16 mm on the largest. The rule comes into force on 1 July 2028, so companies have time to change their packaging. Until then, a pack without the logo does not break this new rule.

Keep in mind: FSSAI's separate idea that non-milk products should not be sold as "paneer" is still only a proposal and is not part of this rule. Whatever the logo, keep checking the ingredients and use-by date on every pack.

Law: Food Safety and Standards (Labelling and Display) Second Amendment Regulations, 2026, amending Schedule I of the 2020 Regulations (Section 92, Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006)
Issued by: FSSAI, notified November 2026, in force from 1 July 2028
