An arrest after 5 PM is no excuse to keep someone locked up till the next morning ⏰⚖️

The Kerala High Court has struck down state guidelines that asked police to produce arrested persons before a magistrate, as far as possible, only between 10 AM and 5 PM, and to do so later only in "exceptional circumstances". The court said the 24-hour limit in Article 22(2) of the Constitution and Section 58 of the BNSS is an outer limit. It does not give the police a right to wait until that time is almost over.

The court explained that holding a person overnight just because court hours have ended can delay their access to a lawyer, their chance to challenge the arrest, and their chance to oppose police custody. An executive protocol cannot water down safeguards given by the Constitution and the law.

The court did uphold the requirement of informing the magistrate in advance before producing someone after 5 PM, and said the State may still take reasonable steps for the safety of judicial officers. The State has been directed to issue fresh guidelines in line with the judgment.
