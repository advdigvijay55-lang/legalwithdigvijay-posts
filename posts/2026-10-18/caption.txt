Does alimony stop if a divorced wife remarries? The answer is not a simple yes or no 💍⚖️

A couple married in 2021. The Family Court in Mysuru granted the wife a divorce and Rs 25 lakh as permanent alimony. The husband appealed only against the amount. While the appeal was pending, the wife remarried in April 2024.

The Karnataka High Court said Section 25 of the Hindu Marriage Act allows a court to change or cancel alimony when circumstances change, and Section 25(3) specifically covers remarriage. Alimony is meant for reasonable support, not a windfall. Remarriage is a material change that must be considered, but it does not by itself end the right. The alimony was reduced to Rs 10 lakh, and the divorce stayed as it was.

Tip: there is no fixed formula for alimony. The husband's other arguments failed because no affidavit of assets and liabilities was filed. Proper disclosure matters.

Case: B v. Smt. A, MFA No. 1557 of 2023 (FC), 2026 LiveLaw (Kar) 421
Bench: Justices D K Singh and H Shanthi Bhushan, Karnataka High Court
