Station ki khidki se ticket lete hain? Yeh khabar aapke liye hai 🚆⚖️

Ab tak railway travel insurance ka vikalp sirf online ticket kharidne walon ko milta tha. Counter se ticket lene wale — jinme bahut se gareeb aur gaon ke yatri hain — is suraksha se bahar the.

Supreme Court ne Railways ko aadesh diya hai ki 27 October 2026 se sabhi stations par counter se bike ticket par bhi online ticket jaisa insurance cover diya jaye. Aadesh mandatory hai, aur paalan na hone par zimmedari seedhe Railway Board ki hogi.

Court ne safety budget poora kharch na hone par naraazgi jatayi aur Railway Board ke Chairman va members ko 16 November ko khud court mein bulaya hai. Premium aur cover amount ki jaankari abhi aani baaki hai — Railways ke notification par nazar rakhein.

Case: Union of India v. Radha Yadav, 2026 LiveLaw (SC) 1047
Bench: Justice Ahsanuddin Amanullah & Justice R. Mahadevan
