Buy your train ticket at the station counter? This one is for you 🚆⚖️

Until now, railway travel insurance was available only to passengers booking online. Those buying at the counter — many of them poor and rural travellers — were left without that protection.

The Supreme Court has directed the Railways that from 27 October 2026, every ticket sold over the counter at all stations must carry insurance cover just like online tickets. The order is mandatory, and the Railway Board will be directly responsible if it is not followed.

The Court was unhappy that the passenger-safety budget had not been fully used, and has called the Railway Board Chairman and members to appear in person on 16 November. The premium and cover amount are yet to be announced — keep an eye out for the Railways' notification.

Case: Union of India v. Radha Yadav, 2026 LiveLaw (SC) 1047
Bench: Justice Ahsanuddin Amanullah & Justice R. Mahadevan
