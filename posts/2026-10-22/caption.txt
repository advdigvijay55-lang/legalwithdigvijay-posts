ESIC now gives ₹20,000 towards the funeral of an insured worker 🕯️⚖️

The Employees' State Insurance Corporation (ESIC) has fixed the funeral benefit at ₹20,000 under Section 32(1)(f) of the Code on Social Security, 2020. Under the old ESI framework the amount was up to ₹15,000. The notification is dated 7 October 2026, was published in the Gazette on 16 October 2026, and applies from 8 May 2026.

Who can claim? The eldest surviving member of the family of the deceased insured person. If the worker had no family, or was not living with the family at the time of death, the person who actually paid for the funeral can claim it. The claim is made on ESIC's Form 22 at the ESIC Branch Office, with the death certificate, the deceased's ESIC insurance number and the claimant's details.

The old rule gave 3 months from the date of death to claim. The new notification does not set a separate time limit, so it is safest to apply early. Remember, this benefit is only for persons insured under ESIC. It does not change ESI contribution rates, and the employer does not have to pay this amount directly.

Law: Section 32(1)(f), Code on Social Security, 2020, read with Rule 21, Social Security (Central) Rules, 2026 | ESIC Notification No. N-12011/1/2023-P&D dated 7 October 2026
