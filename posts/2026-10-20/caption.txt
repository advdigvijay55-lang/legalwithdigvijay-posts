That "70% off" tag on your shopping app may soon have to tell the truth 🛒⚖️

The Centre has notified the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) (Amendment) Rules, 2026. They will apply from 1 January 2027. The biggest change for buyers: when a platform shows a discount, it must also show the prior price, which means the lowest price offered in the 30 days before the sale. So you can check how real the discount is.

Product pages will also need to show details like best-before dates, return and refund terms, warranty, delivery and payment options. Imported goods must show the country of origin and importer details. Paid placements in search results must be clearly marked as sponsored, and platforms cannot manipulate search results to mislead you.

Platforms must follow the dark patterns guidelines, do a yearly self-audit and display a compliance certificate. Complaints must be acknowledged within 48 hours and resolved within one month, and you get a copy of your recorded complaint. Marketplaces also cannot charge bundled fees for unrelated services, except loyalty or membership plans. Until 1 January 2027, the existing 2020 rules continue.

Law: Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) (Amendment) Rules, 2026, Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food and Public Distribution
